# Symbolic Domains

## Supported domains
- Arithmetic: + - * / ^ 
- Logarithms: log10, ln, log base 2, e^x
- Derivatives: sin, cos, tan, sec, csc, ln, log, e^x, x^n, coefficient, product/quotient/chain rules, power of sin
- Integrals: indefinite e^x, sin, cos, tan, 1/x, ln x, x^n, definite power integrals
- Limits: basic rational, sin(x)/x, 1/x -> infinity
- Series: sum of natural numbers, sum range
- Matrix: 2x2 determinant
- Partial fractions: placeholder pattern
- Trig identities: sin^2+cos^2=1, tan=sin/cos
- Word problems: travel, rectangle area, percent, Fibonacci
- Quadratic solver: monic and general ax^2+bx+c=0
- Linear solver: ax+b=c
- Trig values: sin(pi/2)=1, cos(pi)=-1, tan(pi/4)=1, sin0, cos0
